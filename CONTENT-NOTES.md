# Content notes

This website concept was drafted from the supplied Meridian Honors Academy new school design application and logo.

Core source-derived content used in the page includes:

- Mission: preparing students for advanced high school pathways and empowered civic participation through rigorous instruction, structured supports, culturally responsive curriculum, and research.
- Vision: a rigorous and humane public middle school where students are seen, known, and challenged.
- Proposed grade span: Grades 6–8.
- Proposed location: 400 Empire Blvd, Brooklyn, NY 11225.
- Signature programs: Meridian Research Institute, Residency Program, and Civic Leadership Program.
- Grade progression:
  - Grade 6: Community Inquiry / Neighborhood Residency / Community Issues Forum.
  - Grade 7: Applied Research / Institutional Residency / Youth Action Project.
  - Grade 8: Independent Thesis / Career and Civic Residency / Public Defense and Action Campaign.
- Student experience: Humanities Seminar, STEM Inquiry, Research Institute, Advisory, intervention or acceleration, residency preparation and reflection, civic leadership, exhibition and portfolio reflection.
- Student support model: MTSS, advisory, ENL, special education, targeted intervention, and intensive individualized supports.

Marketing headlines and microcopy were written for the website while remaining consistent with the supplied school design.
