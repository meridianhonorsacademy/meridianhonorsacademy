About Meridian

Advanced pathways.
Empowered participation.

Students are seen as individuals, known through strong relationships, and challenged to achieve at high levels.

Mission

Meridian Honors Academy prepares students for advanced high school pathways and empowered civic participation through rigorous instruction, structured student supports, culturally responsive curriculum, and research connected to community and global inquiry.

Vision

Meridian Honors Academy prepares students to:

  • Think critically
  • Conduct meaningful research
  • Engage with real-world challenges
  • Take informed civic action

Identity-affirming curriculum and authentic student voice help every scholar feel represented and respected.

Advisory, mentorship, small learning communities, and family partnership create strong relationships.

Advanced coursework, research, public exhibitions, and high expectations prepare students for demanding high schools.

Why Crown Heights

The neighborhood is part of the classroom.

Crown Heights offers history, cultural institutions, libraries, parks, colleges, community organizations, and civic questions that can become powerful sources of inquiry. Meridian students will study their community as researchers, historians, scientists, and problem-solvers.
