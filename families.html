Community Building Events

A year built around belonging, partnership, voice, and joy.

Meridian Honors Academy’s calendar is built around five components: teacher-student relationships and belonging, consistent schoolwide SEL structures, regular and collaborative family engagement, a predictable and supportive environment, and students’ authentic voice and leadership.

These components allow students to experience joy at Meridian. Joy means feeling known, doing meaningful work, welcoming others, having public moments of pride, and contributing to something larger than oneself.

LateAUG

Community foundation

Staff SEL Institute & Family Welcome

Staff build shared routines, referral paths, roles, and family communication expectations before students arrive.

OutcomeAdults begin the year prepared to create consistent, caring, and responsive experiences.
Week 1SEP

Belonging

Belonging Launch Week

Student and family orientation, school scavenger hunt, identity maps, student ambassador tours, and low-stakes team challenges.

OutcomeStudents learn routines, build relationships, and begin to see Meridian as their community.
OctoberOCT

Family celebration

Carnival

Games, food, activities, and student-led experiences bring families and staff together.

OutcomeStrong family participation and early Student Council practice in event leadership.
NovemberNOV

Crew & service

Crews-giving & Service Week

Students connect and show gratitude to their advisory crews. Each crew identifies a community need and completes one collective act of service.

OutcomeStudents strengthen crew identity and experience service as shared responsibility.
DecemberDEC

Music & expression

Winter Jam

A proposed partnership with Brooklyn Conservatory of Music featuring possibilities such as West African music, chorus, drumming, student DJs, live music, and an open mic.

OutcomeStudents and families celebrate creativity, culture, voice, and community pride.
JanuaryJAN

Technology & play

Tech & Game Night

A proposed partnership with Crown Heights Library and its Teen Tech Center. Students collaborate through robotics, digital design, gaming, and digital citizenship.

OutcomeTechnology becomes a social, creative, and responsible learning experience.
FebruaryFEB

History & identity

Crown Heights Stories & Legacy

A proposed Weeksville Heritage Center partnership or teacher-led experience in which families and local storytellers contribute community knowledge.

OutcomeStudents explore identity, history, belonging, and the knowledge carried by their community.
MarchMAR

Culture & family

International Food Night

Families share food, music, games, stories, and traditions representing the school community.

OutcomeFamilies become knowledge contributors and students experience cultural exchange with pride.
AprilAPR

Wellness & nature

Rooted & Rising

A proposed partnership with Brooklyn Botanic Garden connecting wellness, nature, observation, sustainability, and inquiry.

OutcomeStudents connect personal well-being and environmental learning to the wider community.
MayMAY

Public scholarship

Meridian Research Expo

Students present community research while families participate in games, food, music, and interactive demonstrations.

OutcomeStudents experience a public moment of academic pride and families engage with their work.
JuneJUN

Crew pride

Crew-lympics & Field Day

Teachers and students from each crew compete in games and close the year with recognition, reflection, and celebration.

OutcomeStudents celebrate growth, demonstrate crew pride, and end the year with joy alongside staff.

Student voice

Students will have opportunities to propose new traditions, gather peer feedback, and help lead events throughout the year.
