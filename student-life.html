Student Council

Your ideas can shape the school.

Student Council gives Meridian scholars a real role in building traditions, planning events, representing their peers, and improving school life.

Lead with purpose

Speak up.Listen well.Make things happen.

Student Council is designed for students who want to help—not only students who already see themselves as leaders. Members learn how to gather ideas, work as a team, plan responsibly, speak to an audience, and follow through.

Students can contribute through elected positions, grade representatives, committees, event teams, and short-term projects.

Possible roles

01

President

Facilitates meetings, represents student priorities, and helps teams stay focused on shared goals.

This is for you if...You enjoy bringing people together, speaking clearly, listening to different perspectives, and helping a team stay focused.
02

Vice President

Supports committees, coordinates projects, and steps in to keep work moving.

This is for you if...You are dependable, organized, comfortable supporting others, and ready to step in wherever the team needs help.
03

Secretary

Records decisions, organizes agendas, shares updates, and tracks next steps.

This is for you if...You notice details, like keeping things organized, communicate clearly, and can turn discussion into useful next steps.
04

Grade Representatives

Listen to classmates, bring grade-level ideas to meetings, and report decisions back to peers.

This is for you if...You listen to classmates, ask good questions, care about fairness, and want to make sure your grade’s ideas are represented.
05

Event & Culture Committee

Helps design celebrations, spirit events, service projects, and welcoming school traditions.

This is for you if...You enjoy planning, decorating, creating experiences, solving practical problems, and helping others feel welcome.
06

Communications Team

Creates announcements, surveys, posters, student news, and ways for every voice to be heard.

This is for you if...You like writing, design, announcements, surveys, photography, or finding creative ways to share information.

What could Student Council do?

Real projects with visible impact.

  • Help lead Belonging Launch Week and student ambassador tours
  • Plan the October Carnival and Winter Jam
  • Choose service priorities for Crews-giving and Service Week
  • Gather student feedback about clubs, food, school culture, and events
  • Create spirit days, recognition programs, and community celebrations
  • Propose additional calendar events and traditions
  • Represent students at family and community meetings
  • Help make the school more welcoming, inclusive, and joyful
